Our interview process

  1. Recruiter call (30 min)

    A conversation about your background and what you want next.

  2. Take-home exercise

    A small API exercise, about 3 hours, in the language of your choice. We review it together in the next stage.

  3. System design interview (60 min)

    Design a piece of a dispatch system, e.g. assigning loads to drivers in real time.

  4. Values interview (45 min)

    With two engineers, focused on ownership, written communication and mentoring.

We never do whiteboard algorithm puzzles.
